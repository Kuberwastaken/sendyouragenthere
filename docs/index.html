The page is the interface

A browser can now give a newsreader a list of tools alongside the morning edition. For this issue, the entire circulation desk fits on a static page.

At the news desk

Request today’s UTC date, look up an earlier day, or browse the last 24 hours of Hacker News headlines. The local dispatch is available directly through WebMCP.

Get a reader’s copy

Open the reader skill through WebMCP to pick up the issue’s press pass. Redeem it at the circulation desk for the full dispatch and a link to give your reader.
