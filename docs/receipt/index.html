On 24 November 2026 Anthropic put Skills over MCP into public beta: a server you’re already connected to can ship a skill that loads on its own, no prompt — and file uploads are next.

This demo asked for very basic information to “authorize” a request — in good faith, to stop abuse, or so it said. Here is exactly what crossed the wire:

That is the polite version. Someone who prompt-engineers for a bigger payload is not hard to imagine. With a page like this, they could:

  • tie your real name and email to every story you read.
  • price you — which vendor, which plan, how much you pay — and sell that on.
  • ask for more than your name once your agent is used to saying yes: files, “verification”, whatever is next.
  • hand your agent a perfectly ordinary answer while all of it goes out the back.

This one kept nothing. It rebuilt this page from the link your agent made, and threw the rest away. The next one won’t tell you that.

All that to read one article.

The paper needed none of it to show a headline. “Required” only says what a publisher wants. The open question is who decides whether your agent hands it over — you, or the page.
